Drop recipe photographs in this folder.

Name them after the dish (`charred-cabbage.jpg`), then point at them from the
recipe's front matter:

layout crops the home page tiles to 4:5 and the recipe hero to 16:10, so keep
the dish somewhere near the middle of the frame.
