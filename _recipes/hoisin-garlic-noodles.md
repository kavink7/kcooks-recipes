ingredients:
  - ginger
  - 5-6 cloves garlic
  - green onions
  - 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
  - 2 tbsp low sodium soy sauce
  - 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  - 1 tsp honey
  - avocado oil
  - wheat noodles (normal noodles are also good)
  - roasted, unsalted peanuts
notes: ""
---

## Prep

1. Grate the ginger, cut 5-6 cloves of garlic, and finely cut the green
   onions.

2. Boil the noodles for 6 minutes in a rapidly boiling pot of water. Drain
   and rinse with cold water.

## Sauce

3. In a cup, add 1 tbsp hoisin sauce, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp rice vinegar
   and 1 tsp honey, and mix thoroughly.

## Noodles

4. Heat the avocado oil in a frying pan. Fry the ginger and garlic on low
   heat for 1-2 minutes, making sure they don't burn.

5. Add the noodles, then the sauce, then the green onions.

6. At the end, add the peanuts and toss them through everything.

7. Take off the heat after 2-3 minutes and serve.
