---
title: Thai green curry
date: 2026-09-06
serves: 4
time: 40 min
# image: /assets/images/recipes/thai-green-curry.jpg
tags: [chicken, weeknight]
intro: Crack the coconut milk properly and the rest of the dish takes care of itself.
ingredients:
  - 4 tbsp Thai green curry paste
  - 700 g chicken thighs or breast, cut into bite-size pieces
  - 400 ml full-fat coconut milk
  - 200 ml chicken stock
  - 1 tbsp fish sauce
  - 2 tsp jaggery or palm sugar
  - 6 kaffir lime leaves
  - 2 red bell peppers, cut medium or julienned
  - a large handful of Thai basil
  - 6 Thai eggplants, quartered (optional)
notes: |
  Go easy on the fish sauce. Past a certain point it stops seasoning the curry
  and starts smelling of fish.

  Serve with Thai hom mali jasmine rice.
---

1. Bring the coconut milk to a full boil, then stir in the curry paste. Keep it
   at a medium boil and stir. Watch for the oil separating out.

2. Once the oil floats to the top, add the chicken.

3. Add the chicken stock, jaggery and fish sauce, then the bell peppers.

4. Drop in the kaffir lime leaves.

5. For a brighter green, blend the Thai basil with a little oil and stir it
   through.

6. Simmer for 15 minutes.
