time: 40 min

tags: [vegetables, weeknight]
intro: The cut side goes almost black. That is the point of the dish.
ingredients:
  - 1 small hispi or sweetheart cabbage
  - 2 tbsp neutral oil
  - 90 g unsalted butter
  - 40 g blanched hazelnuts, roughly chopped
  - 1 lemon
  - 1 tsp flaky salt
  - black pepper
notes: |
  Savoy works but needs longer in the pan and loses the sweetness.
  If the butter catches, pour it out of the hot pan straight away —
  it keeps cooking off the heat.
---

1. Quarter the cabbage lengthways, keeping the core intact so the leaves hold
   together. Pat the cut faces dry.

2. Heat the oil in a heavy pan over medium-high until it shimmers. Lay the
   quarters cut side down and leave them alone for 6–8 minutes. Resist moving
   them. You want a dark, even crust, not colour in patches.

3. Turn onto the second cut face and repeat. Add a splash of water, cover, and
   steam for 5 minutes until a knife slides into the core with slight
   resistance. Move to a warm plate.

4. Wipe the pan. Melt the butter over medium heat and let it foam, then keep
   going until the solids turn the colour of wet sand and it smells of toasted
   nuts — about 3 minutes. Add the hazelnuts for the last 30 seconds.

5. Take the pan off the heat, squeeze in half the lemon, and swirl. Spoon over
   the cabbage. Salt, pepper, and the rest of the lemon at the table.
