## Sauce

1. Heat a pan, add the oil, onion (roughly chopped, since it all gets
   blended later) and a pinch of salt.

2. Add the roughly chopped garlic.

3. Add the chipotles and fire-roasted tomatoes, along with the oregano.

4. After 1-2 minutes, add the chicken stock.

5. Simmer for 10 minutes.

6. Cool the sauce in the freezer, then blend it in a blender.

## Chicken

7. Add the sauce back to the same pan and mix in the shredded chicken
   (shred with a fork and knife — this is why thighs are preferable, as
   they're easier to pull apart).

8. Cook for a few minutes, then take off the heat.

## Tacos

9. Heat up the corn tortillas in a frying pan.

10. To build: start with a few slices of avocado on the base, then the
    chicken tinga, chopped red onion, cotija cheese (or crumbled feta if
    cotija isn't available) and cilantro leaves.

11. Serve with cut limes.
