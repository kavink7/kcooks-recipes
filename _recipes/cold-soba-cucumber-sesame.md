title: Cold soba, cucumber, sesame

tags: [noodles, quick]
intro: For the days it is too hot to stand near the stove for long.
ingredients:
  - group: Noodles
    items:
      - 180 g dried soba
      - 1 cucumber
      - 2 spring onions
      - 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  - group: Dressing
    items:
      - 3 tbsp soy sauce
      - 2 tbsp rice vinegar
      - 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
      - 2 tsp sugar
      - 1 small garlic clove, grated
notes: |
  Rinsing is not optional. Soba left unrinsed turns gluey within minutes.
---

1. Whisk the dressing ingredients together until the sugar dissolves. Leave it
   to sit while you do everything else.

2. Halve the cucumber lengthways, scrape out the seeds with a spoon, and slice
   on a steep angle. Toss with a pinch of salt and set aside in a sieve.

3. Boil the soba in plenty of unsalted water, 4–5 minutes, until just tender.
   Drain and rinse hard under cold running water, rubbing the noodles between
   your hands until they squeak and the water runs clear.

4. Shake the noodles as dry as you can. Toss with two thirds of the dressing.

5. Fold through the cucumber and sliced spring onions, then plate. Sesame seeds
   over the top and the last of the dressing poured around the edge.
