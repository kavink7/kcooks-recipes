Slow eggs, chilli oil, sourdough

Low heat, no colour, and more patience than the result suggests.

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 20 g cold butter, cubed
  • 2 thick slices sourdough
  • 1 tbsp chilli oil, with plenty of the sediment
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • flaky salt
serves
1
time
15 min
cooked
30 July 2026

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with a fork until the whites and yolks are just combined — no further, and no air.

  2. Put a cold non-stick pan on the lowest heat your hob will hold. Add the eggs and half the butter and start stirring with a spatula, scraping the base continuously.

  3. Keep stirring for 8–10 minutes. Nothing will appear to happen for the first five. When curds begin to form, drop in the rest of the butter to slow the cooking down.

  4. Toast the sourdough hard while the eggs finish.

  5. Spoon the eggs onto the toast, spoon the chilli oil over, and finish with chives and flaky salt.

Notes

Take the pan off the heat while the eggs still look slightly wetter than you want them. They set the rest of the way on the way to the plate.
