I cook most nights and photograph the dishes that turn out well. This is where
the recipes live once they've settled — the ones I've made enough times to
write down honestly.

Everything here has been cooked in a home kitchen, on a normal stove, with
ingredients from an ordinary shop. Quantities are a starting point rather than
a rule. Taste as you go.
